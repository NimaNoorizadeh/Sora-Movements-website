# Sora Movement design direction

Palette: soft white #F7F6F2 (canvas), deep forest #24342C (type and actions), mineral #D9DDD8 (section fields), sage #AAB9A5 (small accents), ink #252824 (body). Clay omitted to avoid a predictable wellness combination.

Type: locally served Instrument Sans. Medium-weight, tightly spaced display type; readable regular body copy. Hero 64–120px, section titles 36–64px, body 16–20px.

Layout: left-aligned, broad photographic hero with headline overlapping the image boundary; quieter asymmetric sections with varying widths. Compare: a conventional equal split makes the movement feel boxed in; the chosen staggered layout lets the headline extend across the photographic grid.

Photography: natural colors, no decorative filters. Full-body hero crop; wide reformer studio photograph; smaller observational group images. No fabricated instructor portraits. Duplicate supplied studio photo used once.

Spacing: 8px base, 24–64px gutters, 80–144px section spacing. Mobile uses 20px gutters and 64–80px sections.

Interactions: schedule class filtering, selectable session times, accessible booking review dialog, mobile navigation. Native focus behavior and reduced-motion support. No repeated scroll animations.

Critique: rejected beige + serif + clay, ornamental lines, numbered principles, floating cards, and centered layouts. Use strong sans typography and photographic asymmetry; reserve structured dividers for class and schedule data. The hero carries the strongest visual gesture.
